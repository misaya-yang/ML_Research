# Complete Source Archives

Two archives are retained separately from this public working tree:

- The complete research backup ZIP, including the EWT data and the original Pro archive.
- The original Pro reproduction package.

This checkout is the core edition. The theory, finite-state CPU checks, EWT code, and completed results are available locally; the complete archive also contains external data. To reproduce EWT offline, copy `02_cpu/ewt/data/en_ewt-ud-train.conllu` and `en_ewt-ud-test.conllu` from that archive into [`experiments/ewt/data/`](../../experiments/ewt/data/). The six-state finite example runs without either corpus file.

See also the [manuscript version notes](../manuscripts/README.md) and the [combined Pro experiment ledger](../../results/pro/combined_experiment_ledger.csv).
