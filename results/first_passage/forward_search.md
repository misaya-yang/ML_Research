# Forward Algorithms and Practical Limits for Finite-Budget First Passage

As of 2026-11-23, two forward interfaces admit executable exact algorithms and matching query lower bounds. Program generation shows measured reductions in target-model forward work; strong cheap comparators on the same tasks rule out treating this as a practical budget breakthrough. Graph coloring provides a native carrier and also limits the savings available from this kind of skipping at real scale.

## Sufficient information

| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary native forward calls and one separately priced terminal-step operation, which can amplify the one-step success subprobability by the reciprocal of a public upper bound. ## Matched program-generation task

For local MBPP, the first public assertion is the runtime verifier and the other assertions grade only delivered programs. “Correct” means passing those held-out assertions. We used the population frozen before generation and the prespecified sampling experiment with at most four distinct checked programs. This is an exploratory fixed policy for this project; the default delivery in the Power-SMC paper is a single weighted draw. Independent random keys, deduplication, ties, and the original verification order match across implementations.

Full and lazy generation were rerun on the first four tasks with actual measurements: 76, 84, 100, and 105. Both share native SMC rules, KV caching, and deterministic final-stage numerical row shape. Per task they return identical program, tokens, slot and verification order. All seven public checks match the earlier decisions on exactly the same program text. Initial model load took 1.847 s, task and verdict loading 0.0022 s, and total independently timed execution of the seven historical public tests 0.324 s.

## Practical screening of native stochastic repair

ConsFormer first independently samples an update mask for each graph vertex, then pays for a Transformer forward. If a conflict edge exists, success in the next step requires changing at least one endpoint. At the native \(p=0.3\), the carrier mass is \(1-(1-p)^2=0.51\). Conditional on touching that edge, endpoint mask probabilities for (10,01,11) are (7/17,7/17,3/17); its complement fixes both endpoints at zero. All other vertices remain independent at their original rates. A conditional control draw replaces just two mask bits and needs no samples of correct colorings. Use a conflict edge already obtained by the paid current check, or charge a new edge lookup. [ICML 2025 paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/xu25q.html) · [Public controller](https://github.com/khalil-research/ConsFormer/blob/ada4d483c012e937d8d9cbf548ee1ac4f0d67854/consformer/solvers.py#L68-L83)

With one mark and a single hard deadline, even a chain that fails throughout and runs all the way to the deadline saves at most \(0.49/0.51=0.961\) final forward calls in expectation. A stronger baseline examines the full presampled mask sequence and checks whether both endpoints of a conflict edge remain untouched through the deadline. From the topology of 1,200 official \(n=200,k=10\) test graphs, this tail certificate saves at most 12.05 forward calls on average. This is an upper bound for certificates of this particular form. The paper's 10-second single-chain and 50-slot configurations run about 1,490 and 806 iterations, respectively. Multi-slot comparison must use a baseline that already cancels after the first pass; there is no same-machine GPU latency measurement. The official evaluation records whether any solution appeared but does not specify the returned identity for a tie at first success. [Official experiment and data checks](../local_runs/native-consformer-coloring-KktUmH/README.md)

We additionally ran a single-thread TabuCol-style direct coloring search. Saturation-degree greedy initialization and a cap of 20,000 moves or 0.1 s per task were fixed; each of the 1,200 cases ran once. It returned **209/1,200 valid 10-colorings (17.42%)**, independently rechecked edge by edge. Total solver-process wall clock was 31.45 s including reading the prepared edge tables; compilation took 0.65 s, batch load and conversion 2.26 s, and independent verification 0.007 s. Its 209 solutions include all 82 solved by deterministic DSATUR alone. Another 593 graphs have directly certified 11-cliques and hence cannot be 10-colored. The paper reports 11.92% for a single chain and 15.00% for multiple starts on the same public setup. Hardware and timing differ across studies, and we do not have per-task neural outcomes, so no inclusion relation between solved subsets is claimed. Later methods also report higher public success rates. These measurements and bounds rule out large practical savings from failure-tail pruning for this particular policy and task. ## Research conclusion

Finite-budget first passage can be simulated exactly in the forward direction under the stated interfaces, and some paid reads are necessary under the same access model. Program generation demonstrates actual forward savings, but its held-out comparison fails the requirement of additional correct deliveries. Graph coloring supplies a native conditional action without reversal, but savings caps and cheap classical solvers leave little practical room. No single real workload has yet shown all three together: unique problem-solving value over a strong alternative, a high-coverage signal accessible before execution, and net savings large enough to change a physical resource decision.
