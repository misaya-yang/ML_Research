# Primary-source novelty audit (2026-11-22)

- Austin Brown, [Convergence of Independence Metropolis-Hastings Algorithms in High Dimensions](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.34531v1), 25 Oct. 2026. Most important sequence of results is §6 Theorems 7–8. Weights of the two chains are proportional off a hidden set, and the probability that common Q draws visit the hidden set controls diagnostic false positives. Thus rare hidden marks making ordinary paths uninformative are not a novel mechanism. Theorem 1 / §3 addresses sample-run length for high-dimensional worst-case expected absolute objective, not statistical estimation of cold finite-window MSE or source-wise resource caps. The new content of the proposed two-resource lower bounds is quantitative confidence with independent target and scored Q or named probes, and upper frontier, not the generic hidden-set intuition.
- Deligiannidis et al., [On importance sampling and independent Metropolis–Hastings with an unbounded weight function](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.09514v3), §3 Theorem 3.1. Common-draw coupling is all-time maximal with exact no-meeting law. This supports collapse/coalescence arguments, not the marked two-time P-only resource theorem. Their later sections study debiasing IS and compute/moment analysis for estimating P h, not this estimand.
- Smith–Tierney [Exact transition probabilities for the independence Metropolis sampler](https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=e5ed1c35f1b583574dff76722ea2ce6ed8a22ad3&repid=rep1&type=pdf), Theorem 1; Guanyang Wang, [Exact convergence analysis of the independent Metropolis-Hastings algorithms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.02455), analyzes exact rates and uses Smith–Tierney finite-lag formula. Computational n-step law given the population weight integral is not a scored oracle cost bound, but don't claim first exact finite-time IMH formula or first exact lag skipping in all models.
- Chatterjee–Diaconis, [The sample size required in importance sampling](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.01437), and Block–Polyanskiy, [The sample complexity of approximate rejection sampling](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v195/block23a.html), are adjacent finite budget concerns. They address estimating a static target expectation or selecting an approximate P draw from Q, not inferring risk of a fixed cold deployed IMH chain with independently sampled P data and separate counts.
- Meng–Wong, [Simulating ratios of normalizing constants via a simple identity](https://www3.stat.sinica.edu.tw/statistica/j6n4/j6n43/j6n43.htm), classical bridge ratio and asymptotic optimality. Giorgio Micaletto, [Overlap, Not Moments](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.17403v1), Oct. 2026, §3.4 Theorem 13 and discussion, proves finite-sample upper and matching worst-case for particular optimal bridge estimator characterized by overlap e.g. two-point bad examples, but explicitly leaves a minimax lower bound over arbitrary estimators using both sources open. Scope is log normalization, not downstream IMH risk. One should not conflate a lower bound for log Z with the rare Q info necessary for cold risk; the new hard pair directly separates risk and leaves no estimator escape route.
- Strongest skeptical objection: known normalization plus genuine IID P samples is a calibratable synthetic or selected learned-target scenario, but unavailable in many standard Bayesian uses. Original EWT at target cap is an unknown-scale hard-short fixed-window certification workload with a relative risk threshold and observable far from balanced rather than long-horizon absolute risk; new frontier does not yet explain this negative result. Exact two-time coupling skips acquisition but not necessarily same wall-clock or validation stopping reliability. For meaningful empirical proof, build a held-out learned-model finite-risk panel that intentionally occupies multiple sides, reports true margin and both resource caps, and tests known-vs-hidden normalization without offering it to comparator. Existing 0/32 cannot be rebranded as confirmation of the long-horizon theorem.

Novelty finding: most defensible new theoretical message is that revealing a single common scale changes the *joint* resource frontier while not changing the ordinary IMH kernel or prior pooled total-rate theorem. This is a change to auditability, not faster sampling of P. Champion it only after the alternate upper and three lower constraints are verified in the same oracle/window conventions.
