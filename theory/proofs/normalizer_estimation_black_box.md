# Separate two-source minimax floors for estimating normalization

This is a scoped corollary of the same hidden-color technique, substantially simpler than transferring the normalization difficulty to the cold IMH MSE. It proves both source floors with a public normalized uniform Q, matched by the standard bridge upper. It is not a computational lower bound for a white-box potential whose entire table/program can be inspected for free.

## Oracle and theorem

Let Q be public uniform on N named states, R(x)>0 a black-box raw score and P=RQ/Z for Z=E_Q R; η=∑ min(P,Q). The oracle returns a fully scored iid P draw, a fully scored iid Q draw, or an exact score at an arbitrary named x. Counting the latter two access modes together, require deterministic finite caps (n_P,n_Q). Allow reveals of explicit Q value 1/N and raw f=R/N in addition to R, the identity of the named label, caching, adaptive choice of source and point, public palette and support. The problem is estimating log Z to additive ε with probability 1-δ for all N and all equivalent P,Q having overlap at least η0. With universal small constants ε,η0, δ≤.1, a feasible cap pair needs n_P,n_Q each at least c η0^-1 ε^-2 log(1/δ); separate sufficiently large constant multiples suffice. N is allowed to grow with both finite caps, including arbitrary extra samples from the other source.

Both examples randomize assignment of two raw colors across public labels, exactly preserving integer counts. Use rational β∈[2η0,3η0], worlds s=1 and s=1+Δ with rational Δ≘ Cε, s≤1.25. Constants below uniform. The normalizers and hence population risks do not depend on the labeling permutations. Each lower bound grants knowledge of the family and both possible normalizers but not the true world.

- **Target floor, not a P↔Q swap.** Let Q(H)=αs, Q(L)=1-αs, raw values R_H=1/(βα), R_L=1 for rational α arbitrarily close to zero. Z_s=1-αs+s/β, log derivative (1/β-α)/Z_s bounded below near s=1. Q color law Bernoulli on high has KL O(α Δ²). P(L)=(1-αs)/Z_s=Θ(β), with derivative O(β), hence P color law KL O(β Δ²). For small α,β, overlap P(L)+Q(H)≥η0, since P(L) ≥ (1-.01)/(1+1.25/β)≥η0. Choose α after the finite caps to make Q KL negligible relative to log(1/δ). Same numerical raw values and explicit normalized Q occur in both worlds.
- **Proposal floor.** Let Q(H)=βs, Q(L)=1-βs, raw values R_H=1, R_L=γ for γ/β arbitrarily close to zero. Z_s=βs+γ(1-βs), log derivative β(1-γ)/Z_s bounded below. P(L)=γ(1-βs)/Z_s=Θ(γ/β), with derivative O(γ/β); its KL O((γ/β)Δ²). Q color KL O(βΔ²). Overlap P(L)+Q(H)≥η0. Choose γ after the finite caps to suppress P information. The exact same finite dimensions and numerical raw values are possible in both worlds.

**Adaptive named probes.** Under random coloring, after any transcript the unqueried permutation remains uniform conditional on the remaining counts: for consistent assignments the likelihood of each recorded label only uses its recorded raw score, known Q and per-world Z. Thus fresh names are uninformative conditional on colors, and fresh named color law differs from global Q color proportions by at most O(K/N) after K calls. The sampled-color distributions and repeats differ from independent global colors attached to new uniform unused names by at most O(K W_max/N) per call, W_max=max normalized weights in both worlds (finite after α,γ are fixed). Label cache is exactly reused on repeated named queries. Sequential coupling gives O(K² W_max/N) total variation against the ideal experiment; choose N large after caps and rational masses, so both discrepancies <δ. The ideal transcript KL is bounded by the sum of source caps times their per-color KL (chain rule permits adaptive requests). If an oracle estimator is correct with failure δ, testing between ≥2ε-separated log normalizers, its ideal type-I/II errors sum ≤4δ, and binary testing forces Ω(log(1/δ)) KL in this range up to absolute constants. Taking two preselected hard subfamilies gives the two simultaneous necessary inequalities for uniform performance.

**Upper.** Balance at the smaller source budget, or take fixed n of each; let F(z)=average_Q min(1,R/z) - average_P min(1,z/R). This is monotone continuous, with true sign straddling at z=Z exp(±ε) by the bridge identity. At those two *analysis-only* values the mean gap is Θ(ε η), variance of each summand O(η), since both clipped population probabilities are Θ(η). Solving empirical monotone sign crossing, Bernstein on the two fixed values yields log-error ε with n=O(log(1/δ)/(η0 ε²)) each. No scale bound or grid across unknown Z needed. Equivalently apply Micaletto's Theorem 6 at balanced sample fractions with bridge effective overlap H between η/4 and η/2.

## Relation to Micaletto and limits of the claim

Giorgio Micaletto, [Overlap, Not Moments, arXiv:2610.17403v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.17403v1), §2.1 specifies absolute evaluation of unnormalized f (likelihood-prior product), normalized proposal g, and independent observation batches from f/Z and g in Assumption 1. Equation (1) only consumes R=f/g at scored observations. Theorem 13 concerns failure and Berry–Esseen sharpness of specifically the optimal bridge estimator; paragraph immediately after states that minimax over all both-source estimators remains open. They do not formally fix the input representation/query costs in that future-work passage. Our lower bound applies to the scored-batch model, and is stronger in allowing paid arbitrary named evaluations, furnishing f and g separately, and exact public uniform g. It offers a resolved natural information oracle instantiation, not a claim about unrestricted free additional calls to f, known analytic potentials, bounded support size fixed in advance, or reading a supplied potential program. If an exact table is supplied, summation trivially wins. Their overlap H depends on source fractions; for balanced sampling (η/4)≤H≤(η/2). Characterizing instance-dependent sample allocation via weighted H is not implied by this weak-overlap minimax theorem.

Adam Block and Abhishek Shetty, [Partition Function Estimation under Bounded f-Divergence](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.23535v1) pose a different experiment: samples from proposal only and unknown common ratio scale, with an f-divergence or integrated coverage bound. Sharp prior characterization there does not supply the target-information floor or apply under mere overlap with scored observations from both distributions.

## Realistic access and identifiability

- A known normalized proposal, pointwise unnormalized target, and no P score/sampling internals suffice. Q full support gives Z=E_Q R, or if equivalent P then E_P(1/R)=1/Z. Thus the problem is not population nonidentifiability. The empirical shortage concerns rare events hidden from *finite* counts even with arbitrarily many finite draws on the other side. Infinite samples from either side suffice instancewise. Full-support tiny positive raw values are crucial to avoid presenting a trivial disjoint-support nonidentifiability argument.
- [Rafailov et al., DPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18290) Eq. 4 and [Khalifa et al., controlled-generation EBM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.11635): exact reward-tilted sequence target P(y|x) proportional to pretrained reference probability p_ref(y|x) times exp(r(x,y)/temperature). A pointwise raw score can be computed without summing over completions. A separate trained autoregressive approximation to this target does **not** hide its own normalizer; tractable token-level probabilities reveal ∏ conditional probabilities of that specific policy. The paper should label which law is audited.
- Bounded tilt example: use a high-cost exact offline rejection sampler from p_ref with known envelope M (independent accepted outputs) and cheap learned proposal q. h can be prespecified, such as completion class/constraint, polarity, score event. Accepted states alone meet the oracle; if the audit also sees sampler proposals or number of rejections, that yields information not charged here. For known M, acceptance bits have probability Z/M; exposing many such bits directly gives an alternative Z estimator. Report the actual cost of constructing IID exact P for experiments, not just caps, and whether rejected draws are accessible. Do not disguise approximate MCMC or target generator errors as IID P.
- Bayes evidence/physics offers meaningful raw energy scores but exact independent P is often unavailable. Some calibrated private normalized targets/HMMs have accessible Z by design; artificially withholding it is a controlled oracle intervention, not evidence of intractability under the original input representation.
